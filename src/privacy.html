Privacy

Recipe Box at __HOST__

What we keep

Your name, email address, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. The recipes, grocery lists, meal plans, photos and stores your household adds. When something was added and by whom, so household members can see it.

Who can see it

Only the people in your household. Each household's recipes, lists and plans are kept separate from every other household. The person who runs this app can see household names, member counts and overall usage numbers so they can keep it running.

Recipe imports

When you import from a link, a caption or a screenshot, that text or image is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to turn it into a recipe. It isn't used for anything else.

Timer alerts

If you turn on lock-screen alerts, your phone's notification address is stored so a timer can alert you. Turn alerts off in your phone's settings at any time.

Ads

If ads are shown, they are provided by Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show and measure ads. You can learn more and manage ad settings at Google's ad policies page.

Where it lives

Everything is stored on Cloudflare, which runs this app. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete recipes and list items in the app at any time. To delete your whole account, ask the person who invited you or the app's owner.
